# The last ten years do most of the work

## Hook
Put one lakh away at twenty-five, and it earns most of its money after you turn forty-five. [3]
Visual: stat | ₹20.3 lakh | Earned between 45 and 55 | On ₹1 lakh put away at 25, at 12% a year
Image: media/mumbai-night.jpg | Photo: Matthias / Flickr, CC BY 2.0
Source: Worked example, 12% a year

## The boring years
The annoying part is the start. Five years in, you open the app, and your one lakh has turned into about one point eight. [3]
Visual: compare | ₹1 lakh at 12% a year | At 25: ₹1 lakh | At 30: ₹1.8 lakh
Image: media/iphone-hand.jpg | Photo: Global X / Flickr, CC BY 2.0
Source: Worked example

## How it works
The reason is boring, honestly: each year's return joins the balance, so next year's twelve percent is worked out on more. [1]
Visual: list | Why it speeds up | The year's return is added | Next year's 12% is on more | Thirty times over

## A rule you can use
To feel the pace, divide seventy-two by the rate. At twelve percent that's six, so the money doubles about every six years. [2]
Visual: stat | 6 years | Time to double at 12% | Rule of 72: divide 72 by the rate
Source: Rule of 72 approximation

## The doublings
That's two lakh at thirty-one, four at thirty-seven, and about thirty by fifty-five. The last doubling alone adds fifteen lakh. [3]
Visual: bars | ₹1 lakh, doubling every 6 years | Age 31: ₹2 lakh | Age 37: ₹3.9 lakh | Age 43: ₹7.7 lakh | Age 49: ₹15.2 lakh | Age 55: ₹30 lakh

## The fine print
None of this is a forecast. I picked twelve percent to keep the maths clean, and real markets don't rise in a straight line. [3]
Visual: text | Assumes 12% *every year*, no fees, no tax

## Your own number
For your real number, try eight percent in a compound interest calculator: about four point seven lakh in twenty years, and ten in thirty. [4]
Visual: compare | ₹1 lakh at 8% a year | After 20 years: ₹4.7 lakh | After 30 years: ₹10.1 lakh
Source: Investor.gov compound interest calculator

## End
So if you're five years in and bored, that's normal. You're still in the slow part.
