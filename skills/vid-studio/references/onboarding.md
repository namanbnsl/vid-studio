# Conversational onboarding

Onboard the person into an ongoing studio, not a single video. Start with their goals and existing preferences; ask a few useful questions at a time. Do not require a huge questionnaire or manual JSON editing. Use liked/disliked examples to understand taste, not as factual evidence or a copying target.

## Discover in short rounds

1. Purpose, audience, topic domains, languages, distribution, and desired outputs (research, posts, scripts, videos or combinations).
2. Research interests/exclusions, geography, preferred/disfavored sources, angles, depth, freshness versus evergreen coverage, corroboration, uncertainty, time budget and topic-ranking criteria. Keep contradictory evidence visible.
3. Script/post taste: exact sections/order, template files, tone/persona, complexity/jargon, examples, title/hook rules, pacing, length, numbers, ending/CTA, and WhatsApp formatting. “No hook, start with the finding” is valid.
4. Creative taste: brands/audiences, visual references, colors/fonts, diagrams/charts/real imagery, motion intensity, captions, narration, music and sound. Propose samples only for choices that need visual/audio review.
5. Operations: workspace, capabilities, local processing versus storage, allowed services/downloads/costs, monitors/cadence/timezone, recurrence and review presentation.

Do not insist every field be answered. Mark unresolved choices and suggested defaults. Research/monitor setup can finish before video tools or branding are ready; record blockers only for the activities they affect. Configure deferred video capabilities later through an approved setup change.

## Setup proposal and activation

Present a studio summary, editable default profile, additional requested brand/audience profiles, proposed source/topic monitor list, tool/data policy, immediate actions and deferred setup. Everything remains editable. Approval authorizes only specified configuration/downloads/schedules.

After approval follow setup.md: initialize, fill studio.json, profiles/default.json, additional profiles, ONBOARDING.md, brand references and monitor configuration. Distinguish user-supplied choices from agent-proposed defaults. Confirm completed setup before content production stages.

## Return to onboarding

“Change my research taste”, “create another brand” or “make scripts analytical” routes to profile management without restarting. Ask scope only if unclear: project, named profile or defaults. “Always avoid clickbait in the science profile” authorizes that precise saved rule. “Shorten this intro” affects this episode unless made persistent.
